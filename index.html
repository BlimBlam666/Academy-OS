Opening and closing scripts
“The feet begin the fight before the sword moves. Footwork is not speed for its own sake. It is how we place the body where it can defend, attack, and survive. A small step taken with purpose is better than a brave leap into ruin.”
“A fighter who moves without purpose is carried by habit. A fighter who moves with purpose becomes hard to find and harder to fool. Step well. Recover well. Continue.”
