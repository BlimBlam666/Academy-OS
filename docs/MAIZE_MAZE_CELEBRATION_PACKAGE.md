# Maize Maze Celebration Package

The event date is still pending. Confirm it before placing Maize Maze on the dated calendar. This package supports two internal celebrations: Mic's bachelor party and JoJo's knighting.

## Shared guardrails

- Coordinate with the event steward and knighting herald before adding any Academy scene.
- Make every role-play element opt-in and easy to leave without explanation.
- Use inspected Amtgard-legal equipment and current event rules.
- Keep Mic's combat block sober, adult-only, daylight, waiver-covered, and finished before alcohol. Confirm insurance covers intentional padded-weapon contact and the actual property/event conditions.
- Do not turn JoJo's knighting into Academy advertising. The ceremony and JoJo remain the center.

## Mic's bachelor party · The Last Free Contract

Run as a 45–60 minute team quest before any drinking.

### Act 1 · Oath of the Company

Each participant gives Mic one playful “mercenary virtue” for married life—patience, courage, logistics, listening, snack readiness—and ties a ribbon to a company standard.

### Act 2 · Three contracts

- **Escort the Groom:** Carry a soft token through a short course while the company protects Mic.
- **Recover the Lost Shards:** Teams locate soft Soul Shards; each contains a memory or future wish to read aloud.
- **Hold the Threshold:** A brief control-point fight where success is measured by teamwork and clean calls.

### Act 3 · The Last Free Blade

Mic chooses a champion for a light, reeved duel. Win or lose, the company presents a humorous contract releasing him from bachelor service and welcoming the next chapter.

### Non-combat alternatives

- Campfire “true deed or ridiculous legend” storytelling
- A map-based scavenger hunt with no weapons
- Collaborative battle-drum rhythm challenge
- Advice cards sealed in a “Book of Future Campaigns”

## JoJo's knighting · The Road of Deeds

### Before the ceremony

- Ask the herald where drums belong, the exact start/stop cues, and whether a procession is desired.
- Gather short verified deeds or memories from people who know JoJo.
- Rehearse one restrained rhythm and a clear silence cue.

### Ceremony support ideas

- **Drum Procession:** A slow heartbeat cadence begins only on the herald's cue and stops before spoken ceremony.
- **The Road of Deeds:** Friends stand along the approach; each speaks one short deed or virtue as JoJo passes.
- **Arch of Fellowship:** With approval, participants form an unarmed or safely spaced weapon arch after the formal ceremony.
- **Shard of Service:** Present one symbolic soft Soul Shard carrying a written promise that the community will support JoJo's future service.
- **Echo of the Name:** After the herald announces the knighthood, one drum phrase leads a single communal cheer.

### After the ceremony

- Invite stories and photos, but do not pressure JoJo into performance or content.
- Capture the names of people whose contributions should be credited.
- Preserve ceremony media separately from Academy promotional media.
