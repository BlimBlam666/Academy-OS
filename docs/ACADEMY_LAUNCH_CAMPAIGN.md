# Academy Launch Campaign

## Mission

Launch the Academy of Mercenary Arts at the first Wednesday Fighters Practice on **January 6, 2027**, with F104 — The Ready Stance. Use the four months before launch to prove the teaching loop in public, build a small audience, prepare the Black Root reign, and finish a Crown Qualifications portfolio.

The Academy is a training and creative initiative. It should not be presented as an official Amtgard program. Current Amtgard rules, event staff, reeves, venue requirements, consent, and equipment inspection always take precedence.

## Campaign outcomes

By January 6:

- RinCon visitors receive a safe, welcoming introduction to Amtgard and a clear invitation to the Academy.
- Maize Maze includes memorable, consent-based celebrations for Mic and JoJo without competing with the official knighting ceremony.
- Three finished Crown Quals entries are ready, with two optional entries if time and local category rules permit.
- F104 has a confirmed field, instructor run sheet, equipment kit, attendance method, success standard, and content plan.
- The Academy has a simple public promise: learn one skill, practice it safely, use it in a game, and leave with a next step.

## September — Build the campaign

### September 1–6 · Command and scope

- Confirm owners for operations, instruction, safety, materials, media, and follow-up.
- Add RinCon, Maize Maze, Crown Quals, and January launch work to the Command Hall.
- Ask Obsidian Gate leadership for the Crown Quals date, entry requirements, judging rubric, and declaration deadline.
- Create one QR destination for Academy launch information and interest signup.

### September 7–13 · Prototypes and scripts

- Build the first safe prototypes of the Soul Shard plushie, Book of Souls game aid, and Black Root Mask.
- Rehearse the 60-second Amtgard welcome and five-minute safety orientation.
- Confirm RinCon class capacities, volunteers, venue rules, and tool/material restrictions.

### September 14–20 · RinCon build night

- Make one complete sword and one complete throwie as teaching masters.
- Pre-cut and label class kits; keep cutting tools in a staff-only station.
- Test the Academy QR code on a phone and print a small backup URL.
- Photograph Crown Quals prototypes and record materials, time, design decisions, and failures.

### September 21–27 · Full rehearsal

- Run both RinCon classes against a timer with volunteer learners.
- Practice the Amtgard introduction, consent language, and launch invitation.
- Inspect every demonstration weapon and prepare a damaged-equipment quarantine bin.
- Pack by station, not by material: welcome, sword, throwie, inspection, play, and follow-up.

### September 28–October 1 · Lock and load

- Freeze the class plans; do not add untested activities.
- Confirm transport, badges, shifts, emergency contacts, signage, and venue arrival instructions.
- Schedule three spoiler-safe posts: before RinCon, during the weekend, and the Monday follow-up.

## October — RinCon and conversion

### October 2–4 · RinCon

- Run the sword and throwie workshops from the [RinCon Amtgard Introduction Package](RINCON_AMTGARD_INTRO_PACKAGE.md).
- Keep a walk-up demonstration lane with inspected loaner equipment and a dedicated reeve.
- Offer the two-minute “First Contract” micro-quest and stamp completed recruit cards.
- Invite interested people to the January 6 launch; collect contact details only with explicit consent.

### October 5–11 · Follow-up

- Thank volunteers and participants; publish approved photos only.
- Record attendance, material use, questions, incidents, leads, and improvements.
- Send the next-step message within 72 hours: local park, Academy launch, and beginner resources.
- Choose one RinCon lesson to improve before the next outreach event.

### October 12–31 · Build and field-test

- Refine all three Crown Quals builds from prototype feedback.
- Confirm Maize Maze date, site rules, ceremony order, bachelor-party safety plan, and who controls the official knighting.
- Run one 10-minute Academy micro-lesson at each normal park day: stance, guard, clean shots, or calling shots.
- Capture one useful, consent-cleared teaching clip per week.

## November — Maize Maze and proof of practice

- Use the [Maize Maze Celebration Package](MAIZE_MAZE_CELEBRATION_PACKAGE.md) once the event date is confirmed.
- Keep the bachelor-party combat block sober, adult-only, daylight, waiver-covered, inspected, and complete before alcohol.
- Coordinate the drum procession and JoJo tributes with the ceremony herald so the Academy supports rather than overtakes the knighting.
- Finish functional construction on the three core Crown Quals entries by November 22.
- Run a complete F104 pilot with a small group; record timing, confusion points, and the observable passing standard.
- Publish an Academy “What happens at practice?” post and an interest/signup link.

## December — Qualify and launch

### December 1–6 · Mock judging

- Present each Crown Quals entry to two reviewers without explaining it first.
- Collect scores for finish, durability, originality, documentation, and presentation.
- Confirm that entries are separate and eligible under the local rules; move optional entries out of the portfolio if they weaken the set.

### December 7–20 · Final production

- Complete repairs, edge finishing, labels, process photos, build notes, and safe transport containers.
- Treat **December 20** as the internal hard deadline unless the official Crown Quals deadline is earlier.
- Complete the F104 instructor sheet, equipment checklist, safety brief, course link, attendance form, and after-action review.
- Prepare one week of launch content: announcement, instructor introduction, what to bring, day-of reminder, and recap.

### December 21–January 3 · Freeze and rehearse

- Pack the Crown Quals portfolio and the Academy field kit separately.
- Run a 30-minute launch tabletop rehearsal: arrival, weather change, low attendance, high attendance, injury, and missing equipment.
- On January 3, use Coronation and Shadows Gather to make the final invitation and confirm the January 6 location.

## January 6 launch run

- **6:45 PM:** Field, equipment, consent, and course-source check.
- **7:00 PM:** Welcome, safety brief, and F104 objective.
- **7:10 PM:** Explain–Demonstrate–Practice–Correct lesson loop.
- **7:30 PM:** Single-short ditch and water.
- **7:50 PM:** Winner/loser rounds, open ditch, and water.
- **8:15 PM:** Militia Team Elimination applying ready stance and recovery.
- **8:45 PM:** After-action review: one success, one correction, one Sunday goal.
- **9:00–9:20 PM:** Equipment accountability, attendance, approved media, and F105 preparation.

## Weekly operating rhythm

- **Monday:** Review the next calendar brief and choose one measurable outcome.
- **Wednesday:** Teach, practice, apply, and record an AAR.
- **Thursday:** Publish one useful lesson or invitation from approved media.
- **Saturday:** Pack and confirm Sunday roles, props, rules, and safety.
- **Sunday:** Run one short Academy field quest before the main battle game; invite the next action.
