# Academy Content System

## Purpose

Turn real Academy activity into useful, accurate, consent-safe communication without forcing the operator to invent a separate story for every platform.

## Pipeline

1. **Capture** — record facts and an approved story seed during the AAR.
2. **Draft** — convert one source into platform-shaped drafts.
3. **Review** — verify fact, tone, permission, names, links, and claims.
4. **Schedule** — select the platform and publication time.
5. **Publish** — a person performs the final platform action.
6. **Learn** — record meaningful responses and improve the next draft.

## Source package

Every campaign begins with:

- event or lesson;
- intended audience;
- one thing that actually happened;
- one transferable lesson;
- one call to action;
- the consent state.

If one field is unknown, mark it unknown. Never manufacture it.

## Platform roles

### Facebook

Community story, event information, clear invitation, and enough context for someone unfamiliar with the Academy.

### Instagram

One visual moment, one compact lesson, one invitation, and restrained hashtags. Do not let hashtags replace meaning.

### YouTube

Searchable title, accurate description, learning outcome, source links, and chapters after the video is final.

### Patreon

Deeper reflection, process notes, supporter value, and a clear distinction between public information and member-only material.

## Approval checklist

Before publication, confirm:

- names, dates, course numbers, and claims are correct;
- every identifiable person has the necessary consent;
- no minor is identified without the required permission;
- no private roster, injury detail, contact information, or internal dispute appears;
- imagery shows safe equipment and conduct;
- linked pages are the intended official pages;
- the call to action is specific and current;
- AI-written language has been reviewed by a responsible person.

## Automation boundary

Phase 1 generates and organizes drafts locally. It never posts automatically.

Future platform connections must use official APIs, minimal OAuth scopes, revocable credentials, a private backend, audit logs, rate limits, and an explicit approval step. Tokens must never be committed to GitHub or stored in the public web application.

## Weekly rhythm

### Wednesday night

- Complete the practice AAR.
- Capture one story seed.
- Move worthy media into the approved review location.

### Thursday

- Forge the four platform drafts.
- Select the strongest channel and asset.
- Obtain missing consent or remove the identity.

### Friday

- Final review and scheduling.
- Publish only material that passed the checklist.

### Following week

- Record the response that matters: attendance inquiry, useful question, supporter action, or teaching insight.
- Do not chase empty impressions at the expense of Academy purpose.

## AI editing brief

Use this when asking a trusted AI tool for help:

“You are the Academy of Mercenary Arts content editor. Preserve factual accuracy, consent, dignity, and the Academy motto: Developing Fighters. Building Warlords. Turn the supplied field notes into concise drafts for Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and Patreon. Do not invent attendance, achievements, quotes, identities, or outcomes. Mark missing facts. End with one clear invitation.”
