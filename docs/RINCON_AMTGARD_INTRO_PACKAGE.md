# RinCon Amtgard Introduction Package

**RinCon 2026:** October 2–4, Casino Del Sol Conference Center, Tucson, Arizona.

This package introduces people to Amtgard through safe craft, supervised play, and a clear next step. It is designed for Obsidian Gate's RinCon presence and the Academy's January 6 launch. The Academy is not an official Amtgard program; current Amtgard rules and RinCon/venue instructions control.

## The visitor journey

1. **See it:** A greeter offers a 60-second explanation and shows inspected equipment.
2. **Make it:** The visitor joins a sword or throwie workshop with pre-staged materials.
3. **Try it:** After inspection and a safety brief, the visitor may enter a supervised demonstration.
4. **Play it:** The visitor completes the two-minute First Contract micro-quest.
5. **Continue it:** The visitor scans the Academy/Obsidian Gate QR code for the park and January 6 launch.

## 60-second welcome

> Amtgard is a live-action game where people combine safe foam combat, character classes, crafting, service, and community. You can participate as a fighter, crafter, organizer, role-player, or all of them. Today we can show you how approved equipment is built and let you try a short supervised challenge. The local group is Obsidian Gate; the Academy of Mercenary Arts is our training project for helping people learn one skill at a time. Every activity is opt-in, and the reeve's safety call is final.

## Workshop A · Amtgard sword

**Target:** One instructor and one safety assistant per six learners. **Length:** 60–90 minutes depending on venue tools and how much is pre-cut.

- Identify every component and explain what makes a weapon safe.
- Staff measure and perform any cutting that the venue reserves to adults or staff.
- Learners assemble under one-step-at-a-time instruction.
- Inspect core containment, padding, striking surface, tip, pommel, tape, cover, weight, and flex against the current rules.
- Mark the item “training build—inspect before every use.” Passing a workshop inspection does not guarantee acceptance at another event.

**Station kit:** Printed current construction checklist, finished example, pre-measured approved components, staff-only cutting tools, eye protection, rulers, markers, waste bin, first-aid access, inspection tags, quarantine tags, and care cards.

## Workshop B · Amtgard throwie

**Target:** One instructor and one safety assistant per eight learners. **Length:** 30–45 minutes.

- Explain legal shape, padding, stitching/closure, prohibited hard components, and inspection.
- Learners assemble only from pre-approved soft materials.
- Inspect every finished item before any throw.
- Demonstrate at a fixed target first; person-to-person use occurs only in the designated lane under a reeve.
- Teach retrieval discipline: stop, look, retrieve on the reeve's call.

## First Contract micro-quest

- The recruit chooses a training sword or throwie.
- A herald says: “A Soul Shard has fallen beyond the ward. Recover it and return without striking an unready foe.”
- The recruit crosses a short marked lane, touches or retrieves the soft shard, and returns while one slow-moving guide applies light pressure.
- The reeve awards a recruit stamp for safe movement, listening, and control—not for winning.
- The greeter asks: “Would you like the next lesson at the Academy launch on January 6?”

## Additional low-lift activities

- **Name the Weapon:** Visitors name a completed teaching weapon; announce the favorite each day.
- **Call the Shot:** A reeve demonstrates arm, leg, and torso calls with no counterattack.
- **Choose Your Path:** Body, Craft, Mind, Character, and Fellowship cards show that Amtgard has more than combat.
- **Soul Shard Passport:** One stamp each for listening to safety, making, trying, and finding a next step.

## Safety gate

- Participation is voluntary; explain contact before it happens.
- Check venue restrictions, age rules, waivers, guardian requirements, and staff instructions before opening.
- A designated reeve controls the play lane; a designated instructor controls tools.
- Only inspected equipment enters the lane. Failed or damaged equipment goes immediately into quarantine.
- Stop for unsafe conduct, crowding, medical needs, or venue direction.
- Photograph or collect contact information only with explicit permission.
