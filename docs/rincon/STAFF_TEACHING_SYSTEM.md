# RinCon Staff Teaching System

## Roles for every active block

| Role | Owns |
|---|---|
| Event lead / RinCon liaison | Venue coordination, final calls, incident escalation, schedule authority |
| Welcome / queue lead | 60-second explanation, flow, capacity, next-step invitation |
| Instructor | Lesson sequence, demonstration, learner progress, tool boundary |
| Safety assistant | Materials, spacing, observation, learner help, tool support |
| Reeve / equipment inspector | Combat boundary, `Hold`, weapon status, approval/quarantine |
| Media / consent lead | Consent markers, shot list, filenames/notes, privacy, release restrictions |
| Follow-up recorder | Counts, FAQs, opt-ins, promises, AAR facts, 72-hour follow-up |

During an active class, do not double-book the tool-controlling instructor and the combat-controlling reeve. If staffing drops, shrink or pause the activity.

## Academy teaching loop

1. **Gather** — welcome, consent, fit, purpose, boundaries.
2. **Explain** — one practical skill and why it matters.
3. **Demonstrate** — correct example, then one common error.
4. **Practice** — cooperative first, slow enough to succeed.
5. **Correct** — name one success and one adjustment.
6. **Pressure test** — narrow, supervised, and optional.
7. **Check** — learner demonstrates the completion behavior.
8. **Debrief** — reflection, next rep, next place to participate.

## Staff language

- “Everything is opt-in. You may stop or step out at any time.”
- “We are learning placement and judgment, not power.”
- “When anyone calls `Hold`, freeze immediately.”
- “One correction at a time. Control before speed.”
- “That item is approved for this activity now; inspect it again before future use.”
- “Would you like to keep learning with Obsidian Gate or the Academy?”

## Ten-minute success checklist

- Consent and waiver status confirmed
- Loaner equipment inspected and fitted
- Target areas and `Hold` demonstrated
- Learner moves with balance and spacing
- Learner throws one controlled legal shot and one block
- Wound/death response and call are clear
- First exchange stays narrow and supervised
- Learner receives one useful correction
- QR/next step offered without pressure

## Stop conditions

Call `Hold` or pause the station for crowd intrusion, unsafe force, confused lane boundaries, damaged equipment, tool misuse, medical need, distress, unresolved waiver status, or venue direction. Reset only after the responsible lead confirms the cause is controlled.
