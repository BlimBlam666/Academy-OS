# RinCon AAR and Follow-Up

## End-of-shift record

- Date, shift, staff, and active programs
- Estimated booth conversations and class participants
- Completed sword/throwie builds and materials consumed
- Demonstrations run and newcomer participation count
- Equipment failures, quarantine items, `Hold` calls, incidents, or near misses
- Most common questions and confusion points
- Opted-in contacts and every promised follow-up
- Approved media, restrictions, and missing consent checks
- One thing to preserve; one change for the next shift

## 72-hour closeout

1. Thank volunteers and RinCon contacts.
2. Reconcile equipment, materials, expenses, lost/damaged items, and unfinished promises.
3. Separate approved, restricted, and do-not-publish media.
4. Send the Obsidian Gate/Academy next step only to people who opted in.
5. Publish the reviewed recap and January 6 invitation.
6. Hold a 20-minute AAR: facts, success, friction, safety, conversion, next change.
7. Add the chosen improvement to the Command Hall quest ledger with one owner.
