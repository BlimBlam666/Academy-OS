# RinCon Booth and Demonstration Playbook

## The 60-second welcome

> Amtgard is a live-action game that combines safe foam combat, fantasy classes, crafting, service, and community. You can join as a fighter, maker, organizer, performer, role-player, or all of them. Here at RinCon, you can see approved equipment, learn one safe combat skill, and discover how to continue with Tucson's Obsidian Gate. The Academy of Mercenary Arts is our local training project: one lesson, one practical skill, one clear improvement. Everything is opt-in, and the reeve's safety call is final.

Ask one question next: **“Would you rather see how we fight, how we build, or all the ways people belong?”**

## 10-minute newcomer lesson

| Mark | Beat | Staff cue |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Welcome and control | Ask permission, fit loaner gear, set controlled contact, and name `Hold`. |
| 0:30 | Safety and targets | Mark legal lesson targets; head/neck are not intentional targets. |
| 1:30 | Grip and ready | Relax the hand, keep the weapon live, eyes up, balanced stance. |
| 2:15 | Movement | One small advance, retreat, and side step; no crossing feet or blind charging. |
| 3:00 | Clean attack | One readable controlled shot to a presented target; recover to guard. |
| 4:15 | Block | Close one clear line with minimum motion, then recover. |
| 5:15 | Wounds | Demonstrate event-approved arm, leg, torso/death responses and clear calls. |
| 6:30 | Controlled exchanges | Known attack, known defense, switch roles, one correction at a time. |
| 8:30 | First duel | Short supervised one-on-one with a narrow success condition. |
| 9:30 | Recruit | Ask what they want another rep on; point to Obsidian Gate and January 6. |

Success means control, legal targeting, honest wound response, immediate `Hold`, and a positive next step—not winning.

## Demo library

Use the smallest demo that answers the visitor's question. A herald explains; a reeve controls the boundary; performers reset cleanly.

1. **Amtgard in One Minute** — target map, one arm wound, one leg wound, one torso result, reset.
2. **The Wounded Warrior** — show how an exchange changes after an arm or leg wound.
3. **Hold the Gate** — two fighters demonstrate a fixed line, space control, and immediate `Hold`.
4. **Three Challengers** — one experienced fighter meets three sequential one-on-one challengers; no simultaneous melee.
5. **First Blood** — a short first-clean-hit duel emphasizing calibration and calls.
6. **The Three Lives Challenge** — a simple first-to-three supervised duel with a clean reset after each life.
7. **Arsenal Showdown** — compare safe ranges and tactical questions of two approved weapon types.
8. **Spell & Steel** — narrate how a legal class ability can change a combat problem; verify current rules before performing.
9. **Defeat the Champion** — a newcomer attempts one narrow objective against a controlled guide.
10. **The Obsidian Gate** — a story-framed Winner/Loser line: cross the marked gate, land one controlled legal touch, reset.

## Safety and space

- Participant combat begins only after required waivers and guardian steps are complete.
- Until 2026 permission is reconfirmed, limit play to controlled one-on-one or Winner/Loser formats; no chaotic melees, running, or chasing.
- Mark the lane and keep spectators, bags, chairs, and uninspected gear outside it.
- Only inspected weapons enter. A failed or damaged item goes directly to quarantine.
- `Hold` means freeze immediately: no moving, swinging, finishing, or arguing.
- Anyone may step out at any time. Ask about injuries or movement limits without collecting medical details.
