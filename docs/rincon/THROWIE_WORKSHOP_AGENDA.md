# Make an Amtgard Throwie · Instructor Agenda

**Audience:** First-time makers and newcomers  
**Planned length:** 30–45 minutes  
**Staffing target:** One instructor and one safety assistant per eight learners  
**Program time:** Pending RinCon confirmation

## Outcome

Each learner builds an all-soft thrown weapon from approved materials, understands its inspection points, demonstrates fixed-target control, and follows safe retrieval commands.

## Timed lesson

| Time | Segment | Instructor action |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:04 | Welcome and safety | Explain the build, age/guardian limits, all-soft requirement, stop signal, and designated throw lane. |
| 0:04–0:09 | Show the object | Demonstrate legal shape, padding, closure, surface, visibility, and prohibited hard components. |
| 0:09–0:25 | Guided assembly | Issue pre-approved materials and teach one assembly stage at a time. Staff monitor firmness and closure. |
| 0:25–0:32 | Inspection | Compress, feel, flex, and visually inspect every surface. Tag approved items and quarantine failures. |
| 0:32–0:39 | Fixed-target practice | One thrower at a time. Teach stance, clear lane, controlled throw, stop/look/retrieve on the reeve's call. |
| 0:39–0:45 | Close and recruit | Repeat inspection-before-use, invite learners to Obsidian Gate and January 6, then record builds/materials. |

For a 30-minute slot, pre-stage assembly packets and reserve at least five minutes for inspection and retrieval discipline.

## Station kit

- Finished teaching master and current construction/inspection checklist
- Pre-approved all-soft foam/fabric components, safe closures, templates, markers, and labels
- Fixed target, marked throwing line, retrieval boundary, approval tags, and quarantine container
- Staff cleaning supplies, waste bag, first-aid access, and QR/recruitment cards

## Completion standard

The learner can identify why the item is all-soft, follow the build sequence, accept inspection, wait for a clear lane, throw with control, and retrieve only on the reeve's command.
