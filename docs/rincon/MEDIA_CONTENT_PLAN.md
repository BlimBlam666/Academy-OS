# RinCon Media and Content Plan

## Purpose

Capture proof that Amtgard is safe, creative, exciting, teachable, and welcoming. The content should invite a newcomer to the next step: Obsidian Gate and the Academy launch on January 6, 2027.

## Consent protocol

- Identify the media/consent lead at the start of every shift.
- Explain whether a class area is being photographed before activity begins.
- Offer a visible no-photo choice without social pressure.
- Do not photograph waivers, sign-up sheets, contact information, medical conversations, or private screens.
- For identifiable portraits, testimonials, or quotations, record explicit permission and any restriction.
- Minors require the approved RinCon/guardian process; if status is unclear, do not capture or publish identifiable media.
- Consent to participate is not consent to publish. Human review remains mandatory.

## Essential shot list

1. Empty/wide booth before doors open
2. Greeter delivering the 60-second welcome
3. Hands assembling foam equipment with private information out of frame
4. Instructor explaining one safety/inspection point
5. F102 stance, target map, block, or cooperative exchange
6. One complete short demo from introduction through reset
7. Consent-cleared visitor reaction or brief quotation
8. January 6 Academy display and QR call to action
9. Volunteer team portrait with explicit consent
10. End-of-day result: finished builds, attendance estimate, and one lesson learned

Capture a mix of one horizontal establishing clip, one vertical 15–30-second demonstration, close detail, and a clean still for each major activity. Record what the clip proves, not merely what is visible.

## Daily content rhythm

### Before opening

- One location/arrival post: where to find the booth near the north-side exit
- Booth wide shot and day's confirmed activities

### During the day

- One craft moment, one teaching moment, one controlled combat moment
- Short written notes: who/what/why, consent status, and the lesson shown
- Avoid live posting when it would weaken staffing, safety, or review

### Closeout

- Back up approved media and separate restricted/no-publish items
- Record filenames beside consent/restriction notes
- Draft one concise recap; verify facts and links before scheduling
- Carry one strong story seed into the Command Hall Content Foundry

## Platform angles

- **Facebook:** community story, practical details, invitation to Obsidian Gate
- **Instagram:** strongest visual sequence, brief lesson, January 6 call to action
- **YouTube:** complete 30–90-second demonstration or workshop explanation
- **Patreon:** behind-the-scenes teaching decisions, materials, and honest AAR

Publishing is manual. No photo, quotation, participant identity, or private fact enters a draft without a consent check.
