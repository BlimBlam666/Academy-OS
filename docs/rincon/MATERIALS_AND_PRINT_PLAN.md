# RinCon Materials and Print Plan

Pack by station. Every bin receives a laminated contents card, owner, setup photo, and replenishment threshold.

## Welcome and recruitment

- Displays 1–5: Combat in 60 Seconds, Choose Your Class, Beyond Combat, Amtgard Arsenal, Academy January 6
- Projector advertisement and booth-location slide
- Obsidian Gate QR cards and Amtgard at RinCon public-site cards
- January 6 Academy invitation: Wednesday Fighters Practice, 7:00–9:00 PM
- Obsidian Gate schedule card: Wednesdays 7:00–9:00 PM; Sundays 12:00–4:00 PM
- “No experience or equipment required” and waiver/guardian reminder
- Table signs: Ask before handling; inspected gear only; photography/consent marker legend

## Sword station

- Finished master, component example, current checklist, pre-measured build kits
- Approved core/caps, foam, adhesives/tape, covers, grip, pommel materials
- Staff-only tools, eye protection, rulers, markers, wipes, waste and first-aid access
- Inspection and quarantine tags; care cards

## Throwie station

- Finished master, templates, all-soft pre-staged kits, safe closures
- Markers/labels, fixed target, throw line, retrieval boundary
- Inspection tags and quarantine container

## Combat and demo station

- Inspected loaner weapons, sizing/approval tags, quarantine container
- Boundary markers, `Hold` sign, waiver-status method, water and first-aid access
- Timer, demo cards, simple objective markers, Soul Shard prop, recruit stamps/cards

## Staff and closeout

- Volunteer roster, daily run sheet, emergency/venue contacts, incident notes
- Class attendance/build counts without unnecessary personal details
- Clipboards, pens, tape, chargers, power bank, extension/power gear only if venue-approved
- End-of-day inventory and AAR sheets

## Print quantities

Quantities depend on confirmed class capacity and expected booth traffic. Use this formula after slots are known:

- Construction kits: confirmed capacity × number of sessions + 10% contingency
- Recruitment cards: expected daily interactions × 1.25
- Staff cards: one per active role + two spares
- Consent markers: enough for each simultaneous participant plus a clearly visible “no photo” option
- Inspection/quarantine tags: anticipated builds + all loaners + 20% contingency
