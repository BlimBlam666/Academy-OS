# Make an Amtgard Sword · Instructor Agenda

**Audience:** First-time makers and newcomers  
**Planned length:** 60–90 minutes  
**Staffing target:** One instructor and one safety assistant per six learners  
**Program time:** Pending RinCon confirmation

## Outcome

Each learner understands the parts of a padded sword, completes the approved workshop build, and sees it pass through an event inspection. The instructor must say plainly that a workshop inspection does not guarantee acceptance at another field or future date.

## Timed lesson

| Time | Segment | Instructor action |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | Welcome and boundaries | Introduce Amtgard, the build outcome, tool rules, age/guardian requirements, and the stop signal. |
| 0:05–0:12 | Finished example | Name the core, caps, padding, striking surface, tip, pommel, tape, cover, handle, and why each part matters. |
| 0:12–0:20 | Measure and stage | Staff confirm pre-measured components. Staff alone perform cutting or tool work restricted by venue policy. |
| 0:20–0:45 | Guided assembly | Teach one step at a time. Stop the room between stages so no learner outruns inspection. |
| 0:45–0:60 | Finish and cover | Complete handle, tape/cover, labels, and care notes without creating hard edges or exposed core. |
| 0:60–0:75 | Inspection loop | Check core containment, padding, striking surface, tip, pommel, flex, weight, tape, and cover against current rules. Quarantine failures. |
| 0:75–0:85 | Safe handling | Demonstrate ready position, controlled calibration, and the requirement for inspection before play. No unsupervised swinging in the room. |
| 0:85–0:90 | Recruit and close | Invite learners to the booth, Obsidian Gate, and the January 6 Academy launch. Record completed builds and consumed materials. |

For a 60-minute slot, pre-cut and pre-stage every component, compress assembly demonstrations, and finish with the inspection—not optional sparring.

## Station kit

- Finished teaching master and one cutaway/example component set
- Current rules/construction checklist and inspection card
- Pre-measured approved cores, caps, foams, adhesives/tape, covers, handles, and pommel materials
- Staff-only cutting tools, eye protection, rulers, markers, wipes, waste bin, and first-aid access
- Build labels, care cards, inspection tags, and quarantine tags
- QR/recruitment cards for Obsidian Gate and the Academy launch

## Completion standard

The learner can identify the sword's safety systems, follow tool boundaries, complete the staged build, accept inspection feedback, and explain why it must be checked again before future use.
