## Command sequence

### Before RinCon

- Confirm 2026 venue, youth, waiver, guardian, tool, photography, and combat-space rules.
- Assign an event lead, welcome/queue lead, instructors, assistants, reeve/inspector, and media-consent lead for every active class.
- Build one finished sword and throwie teaching master; quarantine any failed equipment.
- Rehearse the sword class, throwie class, F102, and 10-minute newcomer lesson against a timer.
- Print the five displays, recruitment/QR cards, staff field cards, inspection tags, consent markers, and class rosters.
- Pack by station: welcome, sword, throwie, combat, inspection, media, closeout.

### During RinCon

- Begin every interaction with consent, scope, and one clear invitation.
- Keep tool control and combat control in separate staff roles during active classes.
- Use only inspected equipment inside the combat lane.
- Gather no contact information, image, or quotation without clear permission.
- Point interested visitors to Obsidian Gate and the Academy launch on Wednesday, January 6, 2027.

### After RinCon

- Record participants, builds, materials, safety stops, frequently asked questions, opted-in contacts, and consent-cleared media.
- Publish a thank-you/recap only after factual and consent review.
- Follow up with opted-in recruits within 72 hours.
- Hold a short AAR and choose one improvement for the next Academy/Thistle Thief experience.

## Safety baseline

The current official Amtgard rules, authorized field officials, RinCon policy, and venue direction outrank these notes. Until 2026 permissions are reconfirmed, use the conservative prior approval: participant combat requires completed waivers; keep play to controlled one-on-one or Winner/Loser formats; do not run chaotic melees, running, or chasing.

Every activity is opt-in. Anyone may step out. `Hold` freezes all action immediately. A designated reeve controls the play lane, while a designated instructor controls construction tools. No active class should double-book those roles.
