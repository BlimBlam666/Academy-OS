## Teaching and operating doctrine

The resource library adds these operational anchors:

- Preceptor's Manual: short lessons, clear authority disclaimers, one idea and one behavior at a time, observable correction, and repeatable feedback.
- Cadet Fieldbook: Safety, Honor, Control, Attention, and Reflection.
- Academy Corpora: the Academy supports rather than replaces monarchy; Academy courses do not grant official Amtgard authority or rank.
- Rules of Play source: an Academy reference copy, subordinate to the current official Rules of Play.
- Teaching flowcharts and creative assets: source material for instruction and consent-safe storytelling.
- Tabletop Training Engine: scenario-based learning and player resources.

## Authority boundary

Use this order when sources disagree:

1. Current official Amtgard Rules of Play, Dragonspine Corpora, and authorized field officials.
2. Academy source documents in Drive.
3. Existing Academy applications and sites.
4. Local operator notes.
5. AI-generated or templated drafts awaiting human review.
