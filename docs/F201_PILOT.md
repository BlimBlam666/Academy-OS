# Wednesday Fighters Practice Pilot

## Mission

Deliver F201: Footwork 101 as a complete Academy session, pressure-test the workflow, finish the operational closeout within 20 minutes, and produce one truthful AAR.

- Date: Wednesday, January 6, 2027
- Practice: 7:00–9:00 PM
- Preparation: 6:45–7:00 PM
- Closeout: 9:00–9:20 PM
- Course source: Academy Google Drive, F201: Footwork 101
- Motto: Move with purpose. Recover with discipline.

## Required equipment

- Cones
- Foam weapons
- Shields when available
- Clear movement lane
- Attendance method kept outside the public repository
- Consent-safe method for any photography or video

## Preparation — 15 minutes

1. Inspect the surface, lighting, weather, and surrounding hazards.
2. Mark a clear movement lane with cones.
3. Stage loaner weapons and shields without blocking travel.
4. Establish spacing between pairs.
5. Review the session's stop signal and injury response.
6. Open the Command Hall runbook and F201 source.
7. Decide who may capture media and who has consent.

## Practice — 120 minutes

### 7:00–7:10 · Muster, safety, purpose

- Welcome cadets.
- Check physical readiness and equipment.
- State the Whole-Fighter emphasis: Body, Craft, Mind, Character, and Fellowship.
- Read the F201 opening script.

### 7:10–7:25 · Warm-up and prerequisites

- Raise body temperature gradually.
- Mobilize ankles, knees, hips, shoulders, and wrists.
- Review F104 ready stance.
- Review F110 basic movement.
- Confirm that cadets can stop safely before adding speed.

### 7:25–7:55 · F201 instruction

Teach:

1. Footwork serves range, angle, balance, or timing.
2. Feet stay low enough to change direction.
3. Small steps come before large steps.
4. Feet remain on separate tracks.
5. Guard stays alive during movement.
6. A step ends only when stance is recovered.

Run:

- Four-Direction Forge
- Moving Guard Lane

### 7:55–8:20 · Partner practice

Run:

- Mirror Step
- One Step, One Purpose

Permitted declared purposes:

- Close
- Exit
- Angle
- Reset

No striking during Mirror Step. No running. Maintain enough distance to stop safely.

### 8:20–8:50 · Pressure test

Use controlled sparring or a simple militia game. Reward purposeful movement and immediate recovery rather than raw wins.

Suggested observation rule: after each exchange, the observer names one movement that improved range, angle, balance, or timing.

### 8:50–9:00 · Passing standard and close

A cadet completes F201 when they can:

- move in four directions safely;
- pivot to keep facing an opponent;
- preserve a live guard while moving;
- recover to ready stance after each step;
- name the purpose of a movement.

Read the F201 closing script.

## Post-practice closeout — 20 minutes

1. Account for all Academy equipment.
2. Record attendance in the approved private location.
3. Record safety incidents or near misses separately and appropriately.
4. Complete the Command Hall AAR.
5. Identify one method to repeat and one obstacle to correct.
6. Preserve only approved, consent-safe media.
7. Draft one public story seed.
8. Assign the next action and owner.
9. Export Command Hall data if the device is not otherwise backed up.

## After-action questions

- What happened?
- What worked?
- What needs improvement?
- What happens next?
- What public, consent-safe moment is worth sharing?
